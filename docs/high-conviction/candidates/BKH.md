# BKH — Black Hills Corporation
State: NEW_CANDIDATE | Research date: 2026-10-08 | NYSE BKH

## Trigger and facts
Oct 6 definitive Google Cheyenne, Wyoming power agreements through 2048. Total 2.7 GW resource mix INCLUDING reserves; not all BKH-owned. BKH plans $1.8bn investment for 564 MW company-owned gas generation in 2027–29, up to 590 MW grid-connected service and management of 2.1 GW third-party contracted resources through a private microgrid. Management forecasts ~$150m project net income in 2030 and ~$2.4bn unlevered FCF through 2048 net of $1.8bn generation capex. Service expected from late 2027. Google advanced $399m refundable equipment procurement funding. Stock Oct 7 close $75.84 (+7.21%).

## Thesis
Time-to-power monetization through owned dispatchable generation PLUS a contracted fee for managing third-party capacity. Stronger contract evidence than an uncommitted data-center pipeline.

## Comparison
VST/CEG: generation and contracted electricity; BKH utility infrastructure return and microgrid management. GEV/EFXT: equipment suppliers rather than owner/manager. NEE/AEP: regulated utilities.

## Falsification and research
Execution/capex overruns, permits, gas procurement, leverage/equity financing, Google counterparty concentration, contractual cancellation protections, rates. Pending all-stock merger with NWE to form Bright Horizon Energy; last issuer update expected closing by year-end 2026 subject to Montana regulatory approval. Build standalone and pro-forma valuation before comparing.
