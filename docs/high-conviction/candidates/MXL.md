# MXL — MaxLinear | NEW_CANDIDATE | 2026-10-24

**Listed:** NASDAQ MXL. **Theme:** AI interconnect / optical DSPs and 800G–1.6T networks. **Not held** in 2026-10-02 snapshot; not previously in `universe.md` seed. **Closest comparisons:** MRVL (holding), CRDO and LITE (current high-focus). **Status:** research candidate, not a trade recommendation.

## Why now and what bottleneck it monetizes
2026-10-22 issuer earnings and SEC 10-Q show a sharp optical infrastructure revenue ramp as GPU clusters demand more bandwidth per watt. Q3 total revenue $218.057m (+72% YoY, +29% QoQ), infrastructure $124.907m (~57% of sales, +210% YoY), GAAP operating margin 13.9%, diluted EPS $0.30. Q4 company revenue guidance $240–260m. Management reports production ramp of Keystone PAM4 DSPs and customer qualifications for 1.6T Rushmore/Annapurna products; these forward product claims are **not** evidence of guaranteed contracts.

## Economics / valuation / funding
- Sept 30 2026: cash and equivalents $81.085m; long-term debt $164.062m; nine-month operating cash flow **-$17.653m**. Cash conversion remains a major risk despite positive GAAP Q3 earnings.
- Credit facility expanded by $70m to $200m (available borrowing capacity is not cash); inspect covenants and drawdowns.
- Oct 23 close $63.46; indicative market cap ~$5.76bn and EV ~$5.85bn from third-party snapshot. Estimated FY2026 revenue $764–784m using first three actual quarters plus Q4 guidance, implying ~7.5–7.7x EV/revenue. Price may already capitalize growth; compare peer EV/gross profit and normalized FCF.

## Invalidation / research gates
AI optics deployments delay; key hyperscaler program concentration or qualification failures; margins compress; CFO stays negative; stock-based comp/dilution rises; hyperscaler designs move in-house; competitive wins by MRVL/CRDO/LITE. Before promotion, obtain segment/customer disclosures, bookings and product mix, gross-margin sensitivity, normalized FCF and share count.
