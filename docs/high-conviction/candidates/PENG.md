# PENG — Penguin Solutions
State: NEW_CANDIDATE | Research date: 2026-10-08 | Nasdaq PENG

## Trigger and facts
Fiscal Q4 FY26 results announced Oct 6: revenue $566.7m (+68% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $1.00 (+133%). Integrated memory Q4 revenue $340.8m (+158% YoY); non-hyperscale AI infrastructure sales +99% YoY. FY27 midpoint guidance $2.43bn revenue (+40%) and $4.45 non-GAAP diluted EPS ($3.50 GAAP). Company announced selection to deploy and operate a 36,000-GPU AI factory in Norway. Customer's $10bn compute contracts are NOT PENG revenue.

FY26 cash from operations -$151.9m and capex -$11.6m (approximate simple FCF -$163.5m). $750m 0%-coupon convertible senior notes due 2031. Stock Oct 7 close $72.61 (+13.08%). Simple price / FY27 EPS guide midpoint ~16.3x non-GAAP or ~20.7x GAAP; not an EV multiple.

## Thesis
AI factories require hardware integration, memory expansion, deployment and managed uptime. PENG monetizes infrastructure implementation rather than directly financing GPU fleets. Evidence is realized revenue and customers, not only TAM.

## Comparison
CRWV/NBIS/APLD: neocloud/datacenter owners; PENG is infrastructure supplier/operator. MU/RMBS: upstream memory; PENG downstream integration. MRVL: custom silicon/optics vs PENG systems/memory.

## Falsification and research
Negative operating cash flow persists despite profits; working-capital demands, memory inventory cycle, gross-margin mix, customer concentration, GPU deployment timing, convertible dilution and stock rally. Reconcile share counts, cash flow and customer contract economics before any investment decision.
