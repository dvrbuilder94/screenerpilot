# SLB — SLB | NEW_CANDIDATE | 2026-10-24

**Listed:** NYSE SLB. **Theme:** modular AI data-center infrastructure / cooling / thermal management. **Not held** in 2026-10-02 snapshot; not previously in `universe.md` seed. **Closest comparisons:** MOD (holding, cooling), GEV (holding, power equipment). **Status:** research candidate, not a trade recommendation.

## Why now and what bottleneck it monetizes
The **Oct 23 2026** Q3 release disclosed actual Data Center Solutions revenue **$230m**, +76% YoY and +23% QoQ, alongside parent company revenue **$9.449bn** (+6% YoY) and **$828m** quarterly free cash flow. Datacenter revenue remains ~2.4% of total, so this is currently a diversified energy-services investment rather than pure AI exposure. SLB sells modular design, installation and infrastructure integration, seeking to accelerate energized/billable datacenter capacity.

SLB **agreed** on Aug 31 to acquire Kelvion, thermal/cooling supplier, for **$3.4bn cash plus ~$0.7bn assumed debt**; not yet completed, expected H1 2027. Management projects pro forma >$2bn combined 2026 data-center sales and **targets** 2028 revenue **$4.5–5.0bn** and adjusted EBITDA **$700–800m**; these are forward claims, not realized sales.

## Economics / risk
Parent's Q3 FCF $828m is a differentiator from cash-consuming neoclouds. But acquisition funding, Kelvion integration, capex needs and oilfield cyclicality can overwhelm the nascent data-center business. Targeted synergies ($120m annual EBITDA within 3 years) are management estimates; test closing, returns on capital, order backlog and margins before valuation. Do not capitalize 2028 target as certainty or value all of SLB at AI pure-play multiples.

## Invalidation / research gates
Deal fails or closes at unattractive economics; integration delays; customers postpone projects or grid connections; cooling competition compresses margins; energy/oilfield cycle worsens; 2028 segment targets missed. Compare data-center margin/organic growth with MOD, and modular project scope with GEV/VRT. Track financing and debt covenants.

**Evidence distinction:** Q3 segment revenue and parent FCF are reported issuer figures; 2028 targets/synergies and completion timing are management forward claims; upside from bottleneck monetization is inference.
