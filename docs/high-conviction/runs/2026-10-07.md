### MRVL — THESIS_STRENGTHENED
Marvell's October 6 Investor Day materially strengthened the AI infrastructure thesis.

- FY2028 revenue target raised to approximately $20B from $18B.
- Management introduced FY2031 revenue target of $70B-$90B.
- FY2029 custom AI silicon target raised to more than $12B.
- The FY2031 midpoint implies a very large contribution from interconnect/connectivity as AI clusters scale.

Interpretation:

The Investor Day substantially raises the earnings/revenue ceiling, but also raises execution and valuation risk because the stock has already repriced sharply.

Falsification / watch:

- failure of secured design wins to convert into production revenue,
- gross-margin dilution from custom silicon,
- interconnect growth materially below management's long-term model,
- valuation outrunning estimate revisions.
