## 2. Material risk research

### ORBS — new MSCI methodology (sector risk, **not** proven ORBS exclusion)

**Primary fact, MSCI announcement Oct 15 (effective Nov 30):** MSCI will screen companies for substantive operating assets, then five financial ratios, excluding firms that trigger at least four of five. For existing constituents, the screen generally must fail for two recent annual periods (MSCI may use interim data); final affected-constituent list Nov 11. This supersedes earlier temporary treatment of DAT companies.

**Third-party corroboration:** Reuters Oct 16 says Strategy and Metaplanet face potential deletion; SharpLink is among names monitored. This does **not** establish that ORBS is a constituent, flagged, or exposed to meaningful index fund flows.

**Inference:** ORBS's investment-heavy WLD/ETH/private-stake treasury raises a *hypothesis* of non-operating classification and lower index eligibility; **do not equate** that with an imminent forced sale of ORBS. Need check MSCI official potentially-impacted list, current ORBS index membership, free-float passive ownership, operating-assets/ratios under new test, and current diluted shares/NAV. Previous ORBS concerns (historic issuance, controls, NAV uncertainty) remain; October 8 regained Nasdaq minimum-bid compliance should not be misreported as an active delisting notice.

**Falsification:** proof ORBS is not included in any affected MSCI benchmark and/or passes MSCI financial screens would materially reduce this index-specific risk. A verified net diluted NAV and complete cash/debt bridge is essential for valuation.
