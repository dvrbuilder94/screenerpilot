# GEV — GE Vernova | evidence update 2026-10-18

**State:** THESIS_STRENGTHENED narrowly on long-lead generation/grid-equipment demand; RESEARCH_REQUIRED on realized orders, backlog, margins, and revenue.

**Newly discovered (previously published 2026-10-15) primary evidence:** Venture Global filed an SEC 8-K covering a power equipment framework with GE Vernova and Baker Hughes for up to 16 gas-fired power islands, and a GE Grid framework for electrical equipment for up to 21 projects. VG has made initial manufacturing reservations for eight further power islands and five equipment packages. VG expects a first purchase order by year-end 2026 and deliveries under the frameworks through 2034.

**Critical limitation:** framework maximum is NOT firm order volume. ~$17.2bn is VG's hypothetical combined expenditure if all options under both agreements are elected; it is NOT a disclosed GEV order book. Additional purchases require purchase orders/reservation agreements. No GEV earnings estimate, valuation or RSI revision is warranted from this announcement alone.

**Thesis mechanism:** scarcity of turbines, grid equipment and manufacturing slots may strengthen equipment vendors' pricing and visibility as AI power developers seek off-grid capacity. **Falsification:** reservation lapses, no firm purchase orders, margin compression, or customer funding failures. Cross-check GEV disclosures before attributing revenue.
